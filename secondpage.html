    About TRCAC FEEDBACK FORM

    Thakur Ramnarayan College of Arts and Commerce provides a feedback form on a college website is a simple and effective tool designed to gather opinions from students, faculty, and visitors. It helps the college improve various aspects such as academics, facilities, and services. The form typically a section for open-ended comments to allow users to share their experiences and suggestions anonymously. This data aids in enhancing the overall campus environment and educational quality.

    Our Aim

    The aim of anonymous feedback is to create a safe and open platform for students and staff to share their honest opinions and suggestions. It allows the college to gather constructive insights, address issues, and make informed decisions for improving the educational environment, while ensuring that feedback providers remain confidential and free from bias or repercussions.

    Our Plan

    The plan for anonymous feedback should include setting up an easy-to-access platform for submissions, ensuring confidentiality, and encouraging participation through clear communication. Once feedback is collected, the college should review, analyze, and address key issues, implementing improvements where necessary, while periodically sharing updates on actions taken to build trust in the process.

    Your Security

    The security of anonymous feedback should ensure that responses are encrypted and stored in a secure system, with no personally identifiable information (PII) collected. Access to the feedback should be restricted to authorized personnel only, and measures should be in place to prevent tracing submissions back to individuals, maintaining complete confidentiality.
